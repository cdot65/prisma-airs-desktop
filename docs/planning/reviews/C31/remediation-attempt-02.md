| Finding | Remediation | Where |
|---|---|---|
| R1 in-flight rotation lost on context retirement | A Runtime key Version or Delete records the tenant it was made in (`ChangeRecord.rotation_tenant`). When its context retires before it settles (tenant switch, credential replacement), the record is detached like a Gateway change and its settlement still drives that tenant's rotation, with the one-time key and reply dropped (only the read-back key, never its secret, is kept for the rotation). The other tenant sees nothing; returning shows the issuance or removal as established or unconfirmed, reconcilable by a read, and nothing is resent. Tests: `ui_v2_reviews::a_switch_during_issuance_keeps_the_rotation_with_its_tenant`, `a_switch_during_removal_keeps_the_outcome_with_its_tenant` | `change_domain.rs` |
| R2 rotation facts lost on a failed save | Facts the service established (issuance, removal, reconciliation) are saved by `keep_rotation_fact`: a failure keeps them in memory (never rolled back), reports `RotationView.unsaved` (shown as an alert in the rotation panel), and every publication retries the save (`retry_rotation_save` at the start of `sync_changes`; any successful save also clears it). Nothing remote is replayed. An intent's own save keeps its refusal semantics; acknowledging forgets the held key only after the acknowledgement is saved. Test: `rotation_facts_survive_a_storage_failure_and_are_saved_on_recovery` (issuance and removal under `fail_saves`, refused acknowledgement keeps the key, recovery, relaunch shows the original timestamps, one POST and one DELETE) | `rotation_domain.rs`, `authority.rs`, `rotation-panel.tsx` |
| R3 removal opened on a refused acknowledgement | "Application updated" opens the old key's removal only once the snapshot shows the recorded acknowledgement; a refusal or failed delivery shows an alert and changes nothing; an unconfirmed delivery says so and waits for the observation. Test: "opens the old key's removal only once the acknowledgement is recorded" (deniedStore, invalidInput, staleContext, lost delivery, then the recorded acknowledgement opens it once) | `review-sheet.tsx` |
| R4 Harness proposal not routed into the v2 sheet | The v2 Administration watches for a proposal in review whose native review is open, opens the product and tab of its kind and shows the review sheet with the exact draft under that review; the embedded inventory no longer handles it. | R5 applied panel without its receipt | `ChangeResultView.settledAt` is the core's clock when the settlement and readback were recorded (stable across observations). A live or removed result shows Tenant, Resource, Name, Revision (not for a removal) and "Read back at", each "Not reported" when the service did not return it; unconfirmed or failed results never show it. Test: "names what a confirmed change established" | `change_domain.rs`, `review-sheet.tsx`, `client.ts` |
