| R1 an admitted send's delayed entry could settle a later identical unconfirmed send | Deliveries are now correlated by the core instead of matched by text. Each send carries the presentation's own ID (`Intent::SendHarness.submission`, optional, 32 lowercase hex, refused otherwise); the core takes each ID at most once (a repeated delivery of a send it took, as the client's resend after a lost receipt, returns admitted and sends nothing again) and reports the sends it took, with the message each became, on the conversation (`ConversationView.submissions`, `TakenSubmission{submission, entry}`, newest 64, validated). A "Not confirmed" record keeps its send's ID and is settled only when its own conversation reports that ID: an identical message, another send's entry (admitted before or after, observed in any order), another conversation or the transcript's eviction can no longer settle it, and each record settles once. The text-matching machinery (claims, settled entries, entry baselines) is removed from the store. Tests: "an admitted send's delayed entry never settles a later identical unconfirmed send; that send's own ID settles it once" (the reviewer's sequence through the production client, with a remount), and the Not-confirmed tests rewritten on the core's word (lost receipt; two conversations; two identical sends; admitted retry before and after; transcript bounds by entries and bytes); `a_named_send_is_taken_once_and_reported_with_the_message_it_became` (ui_v2_harness, real engine: reported entry, a repeated delivery taken once with one operator message, identical words under another ID as their own send, a malformed ID refused) | `authority.rs`, `harness_domain.rs`, `client.ts`, generated `Intent`/`ConversationView`/`TakenSubmission`, `store.ts`, `harness.tsx`, provenance P50, state-ownership |
