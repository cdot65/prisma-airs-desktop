# C35 — Shell port

Phase: P09 (re-cut, D059). Status: planned. Required predecessor: C33.

## Outcome

The screen matches its prototype board side by side at 1440×900 in light and dark with real core data, as the owner approved from the chunk's composites, and passes its gate at 8.

## The board

R2App, any `start` (home for the composite; palette and tenants overlays). The frozen prototype under `docs/architecture/ui-v2/prototype/` is the specification; `docs/planning/p09-recut.md` states the bar.

## Regions to port

the 244px sidebar (tenant button with avatar, nav with expandable product children and badges, status block with read status, Settings, theme, user); the 56px header bar (page title 16px/700, 260px "Search or go to… ⌘K" button, Explain switch bound to `showCalls`, Harness button); page padding 32/44 with 26px titles; the tenant menu (360px) and the palette (640px); tokens corrected (base text 13px, grid gaps 16–20px, scrims rgba(0,0,0,0.22–0.34)) and added (header height, section heading, secondary text, overlay widths); the Classic switch moved to Settings.

## Parity
