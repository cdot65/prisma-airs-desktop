---
name: grill-me
description: A relentless interview to sharpen a plan, decision or design before acting on it. Use when the owner asks to be grilled, says /grill-me, or before planning a phase or a chunk whose shape is not settled.
---

Interview the owner relentlessly until you reach a shared understanding. Map the plan as a **design tree**: every decision branches into the decisions that hang off it.

Work the tree in **rounds**. The **frontier** is every decision whose prerequisites are already settled: the questions you can ask now without guessing at answers you have not heard yet. Ask the whole frontier in one round, numbered.

Format a round like so:

```
❓ **Q1** - **<question title>**: <question body, may be several paragraphs, including the options>

➡️ <your recommended answer>

---

❓ **Q2** - **<question title>**: <question body>

➡️ <your recommended answer>
```

Word each question so "yes" accepts your recommended answer.

Each round the owner answers reshapes the tree: settled decisions push the frontier outward and unblock questions that depended on them. Recompute the frontier and ask the next round. A question whose answer depends on another still-open question waits for the next round.

Finding facts is your job, never the owner's. When a frontier question needs a fact from the environment (files, tools, docs), look it up yourself first; never ask the owner for anything you could find.

The session is done when the frontier is empty: every branch visited, nothing silently assumed. Do not act on it until the owner confirms you have reached a shared understanding. Then record the outcome (a decision, a brief, a plan) and start the work.
